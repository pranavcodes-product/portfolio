Customer discovery agent

Turn customer calls into requirements

Add your own call notes or transcripts. Loopline pulls out pains, requests and verbatim quotes with who said them, drafts a PRD or BRD from only what was said, and turns each request into a ticket you can paste into any tracker. Anything the notes do not answer becomes an open question.

The live agent switches on once the site's AI endpoint is set. Until then you can add and review your notes here, but nothing is extracted.

    How it works

    ReadYour browser reads TXT, Markdown, DOCX, PDF, VTT and SRT files. The files themselves never leave your device.
    ExtractUp to 16,000 characters of text go to the site's Cloudflare Worker, which calls a Groq-hosted model (Llama 3.3 70B by default). The API key lives only in the Worker.
    VerifyQuotes and evidence are kept only if they appear word for word in your notes. Missing details become open questions instead of guesses.
    Hand offCopy or download the PRD or BRD as Markdown, and copy tickets or download a CSV for any tracker.

    Nothing is saved on a server or in your browser. Live two-way sync with a tracker would need an OAuth app per tool and a backend to hold tokens, which this static page deliberately does not have, so the hand-off is copy and file based.

    Why Loopline exists

    Discovery evidence is scattered across call recordings, research notes, tickets and slide decks, and the customers who gave the feedback rarely see what happened to it. Loopline keeps the path from a call to a requirement short and traceable: every request carries the words that justify it.
